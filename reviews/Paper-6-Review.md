## Critical Findings

### C1. The dark-matter master equation is the second-sound speed, not a first-sound correction
- **Location:** §1.3 Dark Matter, Eq. (eq:first-sound), L240–252; propagates through L269–390, L1229–1232, L1349–1354, L1661–1668, and the abstract (L53–55).
- **Claim:** "the first-sound speed … is not simply c = √(K_A/ρ_A) but receives a thermal correction from the normal component: c₁² = c² + Ts²ρ_s/(c_vρ_n)", with "for a pure phonon gas the correction is c²/3."
- **Why wrong:** Ts²ρ_s/(c_vρ_n) is the standard Landau–Khalatnikov formula for the **second-sound** speed squared, c₂². The paper's own diagnostic confirms the mix-up: c²/3 in the phonon regime is the textbook second-sound limit (c₂ → c₁/√3), not a first-sound shift. In the two-fluid dispersion quartic, u⁴ − u²(c₁₀² + c₂₀²) + c_T²c₂₀² = 0, the *sum* of the squared roots is c₁₀² + c₂₀², but the individual roots are u₊² ≈ c₁₀² and u₋² ≈ c₂₀², with cross-coupling proportional to (c_p/c_v − 1) (thermal-expansion corrections). The paper has promoted the sum rule (or the second-sound formula) into the first-sound root.
- **Evidence/recomputation:** (i) Empirical: in He II at ~1–1.5 K (phonon-dominated), the paper's formula predicts first sound at √(4/3) × 238 ≈ 275 m/s, a +15% shift; measured first sound is 238 m/s, within <0.5% of the T→0 value, while second sound is separately observed at ~140→20 m/s. The formula is directly falsified by the very system (He-4) the paper repeatedly leans on. (ii) For the Aether, c_p/c_v − 1 is of order P_th/K_A, which the paper itself puts at ~10⁻³⁸ (L161, L207) — so the *actual* Landau first-sound thermal shift is ≲10⁻³⁸·(c₂/c)², roughly 30+ orders of magnitude below the required Δc₁²/c² ~ 10⁻⁶.
- **Impact:** Everything downstream — Eq. (eq:vorbit-DM), the flat-rotation-curve profile, SIS lensing consistency, the cluster scaling, the Bullet Cluster narrative, the Tully–Fisher discussion, and the "phonon-gas 10⁻¹⁴ / ideal-BEC 10⁻⁴" bracketing — quantifies gradients of a speed that does not exist in the two-fluid model at the claimed magnitude. Photons and matter would additionally have to ride this (nonexistent) shifted mode.
- **Fix direction:** Either rebuild on the genuine second-sound/counterflow sector (temperature waves *do* have speed c₂² = Ts²ρ_s/(c_vρ_n), but then the coupling of photon propagation and orbital dynamics to c₂ must be derived, not asserted), or adopt the Berezhiani–Khoury phonon-mediated-force channel outright. The claim that the Landau thermodynamics gives the sign "automatically" must be dropped; nothing survives at the stated magnitude within Landau theory.

### C2. Hubble-tension baseline: the "Friedmann deficit" is a sign/double-counting error; corrected, η ≈ 0.11 not 0.22
- **Location:** §1.7, L543–563 (Eq. eq:vpec and following), Eq. (eq:H0-local) L601–608, η at L617–629, decomposition table L662–675.
- **Claim:** Void outflow contributes +3.0 km/s/Mpc but the matter deficit *lowers* the local Friedmann rate by −2.6 ("less matter → lower H²"), the two "nearly cancel" (net +0.4, "well established"), so the thermal excess η≈0.22 must supply +5.2 of the +5.6 discrepancy.
- **Why wrong:** An underdense region expands **faster** than the mean, not slower. By Birkhoff/separate-universe energy conservation, removing matter from a comoving region while keeping its initial expansion introduces a positive effective curvature term Ω_k,eff ≈ Ω_mδ in the local Friedmann equation; H_local² ∝ Ω_m(1−δ) + Ω_mδ + Ω_Λ leaves H nearly unchanged at fixed epoch, and the *evolved* effect of reduced deceleration is exactly the linear-theory result ΔH/H = +(1/3)f δ — the same +3.0 the paper books as "peculiar velocity." The peculiar-velocity outflow and the modified local expansion rate are two descriptions of one effect; Eq. (eq:H0-local) counts the outflow once with the right sign and once with the wrong sign. The literature statement being invoked (local voids cannot resolve the tension in ΛCDM — Kenworthy et al. 2019, Camarena & Marra) rests on (a) a 300-Mpc, δ≈0.2–0.5 void being essentially impossible in ΛCDM and (b) SN samples extending beyond the void, *not* on an internal cancellation between outflow and a lowered Friedmann rate.
- **Recomputation:** Internal arithmetic verified: with the paper's (incorrect) baseline, η = 0.2205 and the robustness claim holds (η = 0.216–0.231 for δ = 0.30–0.15). With the curvature term restored (correct baseline: void effect ≈ +3.0 total), matching 73.0 requires η ≈ 0.113 — half the headline value.
- **Impact:** η≈0.22 is exported to the ISW section (L764, L781), the T_void/T_wall≈2.5 ratio (which becomes ≈1.7 with η≈0.11 under the same P∝T bookkeeping), and Papers 1, 6(a), 6(b). The table's conclusion "the thermal pressure enhancement does essentially all of the work" inverts the truth: the standard void outflow supplies over half of the required +5.6.
- **Fix direction:** Use the linear-theory form — H_local = H_avg(1 + fδ/3)·√((Ω_m + Ω_Λ(1+η))/(Ω_m+Ω_Λ)) — i.e., the void's entire gravitational effect is the single +fδ/3 outflow term (≈ +3.0), with no separate "Friedmann deficit"; the thermal term then supplies the remaining +2.6, giving η ≈ 0.11. Re-fit η, and re-derive the temperature ratio and every downstream use. *(Revised 2026-07-07 by verification pass: the originally suggested alternative replacement √(Ω_m(1−δ) + Ω_mδ + Ω_Λ(1+η)) with no peculiar-velocity term was self-inconsistent — it zeroes the void effect entirely and yields η ≈ 0.247, contradicting the corrected η ≈ 0.11; removed. ## Hawking Section Assessment (§2.1, L800–860)

**What is right.** The Hawking temperature formula (L806) is correct. The inflow-gradient computation is exact: for v_in = √(2GM/r), |dv_in/dr| at r_s = 2GM/c² is c³/4GM (verified). The qualitative mechanism — outgoing restoring pulses impeded/swept back by near-c inflow, with the horizon-straddling asymmetry producing net outward flux — is the standard heuristic picture of acoustic Hawking radiation in a Painlevé–Gullstrand-type flow, and the framework's inflow profile *is* the PG velocity field, so the analogy is structurally sound. Notably, the paper undersells its own result: the acoustic surface gravity κ = c·|dv_in/dr|_h = c⁴/4GM equals the GR surface gravity exactly, so T = ħκ/(2πck_B) reproduces the full Hawking coefficient ħc³/(8πGMk_B), not merely T ∝ 1/M. The "consistent with Hawking's prediction that T ∝ 1/M" claim (L843–844) could legitimately be strengthened to exact agreement — one of the few places the paper underclaims.

**What is wrong or missing.**
1. **Thermality is not derived.** The asymmetric-binding argument produces a classical, deterministic energy flux proportional to the gradient. Hawking radiation's defining features — a thermal spectrum with the specific temperature, arising from quantum mode conversion at the horizon — are asserted by association with the gradient, not derived. "Steeper gradients → hotter radiation" is dimensional analysis, not a spectrum.
2. **Hawking≡Unruh is argued via a non sequitur.** L847–853 claims an observer accelerating through the Aether "creates an effective inflow gradient." Uniform acceleration through a homogeneous medium produces *no* velocity gradient in the medium; the Rindler horizon is an observer-frame construct, not a flow feature. In analog gravity the two effects are related but distinct (Unruh response of a detector moving in a BEC involves the detector's worldline, not a medium gradient). The flat identification "the Hawking effect and the Unruh effect are the same phenomenon" (L850–851, repeated L1175–1177) is an overstatement even within the framework, and the mechanism offered for the Unruh side is unjustified.
3. **Emitted species unidentified.** The restoring-pulse channel is the density/first-sound (i.e., in this framework, gravitational-wave) channel. Standard Hawking radiation for astrophysical black holes is dominated by photons and gravitons with calculable greybody factors. The paper never says whether its Hawking flux emerges as pressure pulses (GW-like), photons (spin waves), or both — a framework-distinctive, in-principle-testable question left untouched.
4. **"Not merely an analogy … the same physical process" (L858–860)** converts a structural correspondence into an identity without addressing what differs: the BEC experiments concern phonons on a condensate with a known dispersion relation and measured T_H ~ nK; asserting identity requires showing the Aether's dispersion (including the K_spin transverse sector and any roton minimum, both invoked elsewhere) preserves the thermal result — the well-known trans-Planckian/dispersion robustness question, unmentioned.
5. **Contested experimental status presented as settled.** Steinhauer 2016 (entanglement) and Muñoz de Nova et al. 2019 (thermality) are real and correctly cited, but their interpretation has been publicly disputed (statistical significance and stimulated-vs-spontaneous concerns raised by Leonhardt and others). "Confirmed" (L810, L856–860) should be "observed, with ongoing debate."
6. **Seeded Schwinger note (from Paper 3 review):** confirmed — this section is the series' only Hawking-mechanism content, and the Schwinger mechanism appears nowhere in Paper 6 (or the series) despite Paper 1's references to it.

**Net:** MODERATE overall (Mo9): the section's core acoustic-horizon physics is the strongest cosmology-adjacent material in the paper and the gradient math is exact; the failures are of overclaim (Unruh identity, "same process") and omission (thermality, species, dispersion robustness), not of arithmetic.
